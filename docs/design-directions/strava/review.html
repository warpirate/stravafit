StravaFit, “strava” direction

Final design (ADR-014): the look and feel of the Strava Android app in our own drawing. Orange #FC5200, Figtree, flat edge-to-edge cards, shadows only over maps. Every icon, avatar and map is ours, and the social action is High-five. Shipped through GitHub releases.

Shape, space, elevation

  • 4pt grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32. The screen gutter is 16. Feed cards run edge to edge with an 8px gap band between them.
  • Radii: 0 for cards, maps and photos; 4 for buttons and inputs; 8 for thumbnails; 16 for the tops of sheets and panels; full for avatars, chips, round buttons and Record.
  • Heights: status bar 30, top bar 52, bottom nav 60 plus a 20 gesture area, action row 48, touch targets at least 44.
  • Surfaces are flat. Only chips, round buttons, the search pill and sheets over a map get a shadow. Start, Stop and Finish get only a 1px neutral shadow, never a glow.
  • Small orange text always uses accent-text. White on #FC5200 appears only as an icon or a label of at least 19px/800.
