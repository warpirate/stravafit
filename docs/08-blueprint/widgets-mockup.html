StravaFit · Home-screen widget

One widget.
Do it without opening the app.

A widget is a remote control, not a second copy of the app. It covers the three moments when opening the app is friction: before a run, during a run, and right after a run. Its layout follows that state. Everything else stays in the app.

1Widget
3Sizes
3States
BeforeReady

A dry-coach line from your real data: your usual slot, weather, daylight, the gap to your goal. It suggests one run, and one tap starts it after a 3-second countdown you can cancel. The app stays closed.

DuringRecording

Time, distance and pace. Pause, resume, lap and finish right from the home screen. Finish needs a second tap within 3 s.

AfterDone

The run is already saved with an automatic title. One tap shares the route sticker. The widget goes back to Ready after 6 hours or when you tap ✕.

Principles

Every pixel earns a tap

A number is shown only if it helps the next action: GPS before a start, pace during the run, the result before sharing.

Hard to trigger by accident

Start has a 3-second countdown you can cancel. Finish needs a second tap. Discarding a run happens only inside the app.

Real data only

No sample numbers, not even in the picker preview. When GPS has no fix, the widget says so plainly.

Private by default

The Done route goes through the share renderer, so privacy zones and hidden start or end are applied before anything is drawn.
